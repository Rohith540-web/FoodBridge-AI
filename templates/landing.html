How FoodBridge AI Works

A seamless process to rescue food and save the planet.

1. Donate Surplus

Restaurants & hotels list their fresh, surplus food quickly using our smart platform.

2. AI Matching

Our AI matches donations with the nearest NGOs based on real-time demand.

3. Volunteer Rescue

Volunteers are routed via optimized maps to deliver food safely with QR tracking.

Powered by AI

Intelligent Food Rescue Technology

Our AI engine analyses every donation in real-time to maximise impact and minimise waste.

Freshness Prediction

Exponential-decay model scores food freshness 0–100 based on category and preparation time. Only safe food reaches beneficiaries.

Accuracy94%
Demand Matching

Analyses pickup location to score demand. Shelters, hospitals and schools get highest priority for faster food rescue.

Match Rate89%
Priority Scoring

Combines freshness (60%) and demand (40%) into a 0–100 priority score. NGOs see the most urgent donations first.

Time Saved~40%
QR Verification

Every donation gets a unique QR code. Volunteers scan at pickup and delivery — creating a tamper-proof chain of custody.

Fraud Prevention100%
Smart Assignment

Volunteers are auto-assigned based on trust score and delivery history — not random selection.

Assignment SpeedInstant
Impact Analytics

Real-time dashboards track meals saved, CO2 reduced, and delivery timelines for every stakeholder.
